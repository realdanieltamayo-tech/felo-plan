## Market right now (Oct 2026)
- GPUs far above launch price: RTX 5070 ~$840–920 new (MSRP $549); RTX 5080 ~$1,250–2,000 (MSRP $999, near $2,000 at big US stores);
  RTX 5090 ~$4,600–5,000 cheapest new, many listings $7,000–9,500 (MSRP $1,999); some board makers report they cannot get 5090 chips.
- RTX 4080 / 4090 are no longer made: only used or leftover stock. Used 4090 sells ~$2,000–2,300 on eBay (asking ~$3,000); used 4080 ~$850–1,100.
- Memory shortage: 32GB DDR5-6000 ~$430–450 (was $100–200 a year ago). SSD prices also rising.
- CPUs normal: Ryzen 7 9800X3D ~$415–470, Ryzen 7 7800X3D ~$325–360.
- Competitor prebuilts: RTX 5070 PCs $1,699 (MSI, cheapest found) – $2,455 (CyberPowerPC); RTX 5080 PCs ~$2,990–3,700 (Aug 2026).

## What this means
1. New cards bought at store prices and resold make almost nothing (1–2%), and customers can buy the same card on Amazon.
2. Builds make ~8–9% at store prices. Real profit needs distributor pricing: with an assumed ~12% lower parts cost (NOT verified —
   depends on the account), the same prices give ~18% (~$426 / $659 / $1,559 per build).
3. Used 4080/4090 can make 8–11%, but need testing, honest "used" labels and a warranty we stand behind.
